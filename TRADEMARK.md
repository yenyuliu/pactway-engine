# Pactway Trademark Policy

This policy protects the meaning of Pactway trust marks while keeping the
Apache-2.0 code and protocol easy to adopt.

This is project policy, not legal advice. Before public launch, have counsel
review trademark ownership, registration strategy, certification-mark mechanics,
and jurisdiction-specific requirements.

## Code license vs marks

The Pactway Engine code is licensed under Apache-2.0. That license lets people
use, modify, distribute, and build commercial products with the code, subject to
the license terms.

Apache-2.0 does not grant broad rights to use Pactway names, logos, service
marks, certification marks, or product names. Those marks are governed by this
policy.

## Marks

Reserved Pactway marks include:

- `Pactway`
- `Pactway Engine`
- `Pactway Delegation Protocol`
- `Delegation Checkpoint`
- `Pactway Compatible`
- `Pactway Certified`
- `Pactway Gateway Certified`
- `Pactway Verified`

Logo files, badges, seals, and certification artwork are also reserved marks.

## Allowed use without permission

You may use the word `Pactway` in ordinary, truthful, non-confusing ways:

- To identify the origin of this open-source project.
- To link to the project or protocol documentation.
- To describe compatibility factually, such as:
  - `implements the Pactway Delegation Protocol`
  - `uses the Pactway Engine`
  - `passes the Pactway Core conformance profile`
- To state that your product integrates with Pactway, if that is true.

These uses must not imply sponsorship, certification, verification, or official
endorsement.

## Restricted use

You may not use Pactway marks in a way that suggests official trust status
unless the applicable process has been completed.

Restricted claims include:

- `Pactway Compatible`
- `Pactway Certified`
- `Pactway Gateway Certified`
- `Pactway Verified`
- `official Pactway gateway`
- `Pactway approved`
- `Pactway-certified business`

You also may not:

- Name a fork or product in a way that is confusingly similar to Pactway.
- Use Pactway logos or badge artwork without permission.
- Claim a business is `Pactway Verified` because its software passed protocol
  conformance.
- Claim certification after modifying the certified implementation unless the
  modified version is re-certified.
- Use marks to imply paid placement, ranking advantage, or endorsement.

## Compatibility language

If you pass a public conformance profile but have not been certified, use factual
language:

```text
This implementation passes the Pactway Core conformance profile.
```

Do not use:

```text
Pactway Certified
Pactway Verified
Official Pactway
```

## Forks and modified distributions

Forks may describe their origin:

```text
Forked from Pactway Engine.
```

Forks must use a distinct name for the modified product unless written permission
is granted. Modified distributions should make it clear that they are not the
official Pactway Engine unless they are distributed by the Pactway project.

## Certification marks

Certification marks are earned by meeting published technical criteria and, when
applicable, operational review. Certification is separate from the Apache-2.0
license and may require a separate agreement.

See `CERTIFICATION.md` and `CONFORMANCE.md`.

## Enforcement

The goal is clarity, not gatekeeping. The project may ask users to correct
confusing or misleading uses of Pactway marks. Serious or repeated misuse may
lead to public clarification, revocation of certification, or legal action.
